The native JacPython build now routes writes to process-wide C-port statics
through owner-provided setters (`set_sock_cloexec_works`, `set_netdb_lock`,
`set_mpd_memory_hooks`, `set_mpd_trap_handler`) instead of storing to
wire-imported globs, so the SOCK_CLOEXEC probe result, the netdb lookup lock,
and libmpdec allocator/trap hooks stay shared across modules, and the
`jacpython` native lane passes its wiring checks.
